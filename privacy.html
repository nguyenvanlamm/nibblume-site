Privacy Policy

Nibblume · last updated October 24, 2026

Nibblume is a game for iPhone. It does not ask who you are, and it does not collect or sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: stars and best score for each dive, your Ink Veils, Spark Charges and Glimmer.
  • Your settings: whether sound is on.
  • Whether you have bought Full Descent.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

The first zone, Twilight (12 dives), is free. Full Descent and the optional booster packs (Glow Pouch, Glow Chest) are handled entirely by the App Store under Apple's own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
