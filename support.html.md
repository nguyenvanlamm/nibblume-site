## How to play

- **Swim:** drag anywhere on the screen.
- **Glow:** hold the Glow button (bottom right) or put down a second finger. Small fish swim to your light, but hunters with red eyes see it too. The belly lights under Glow drain while you glow and refill in the dark; when they run out, the light stays off until they recover.
- **Hide:** let go of Glow. A hunter stares before it dashes; in the dark it loses you.
- **Grow:** eat fish smaller than you to fill the three arcs around your fish. A bite above size 1 costs a size, not the dive.
- **The Leviathan:** on the last dive of each zone a band of water is warned before it is swept. Be dark when it passes.
- **Boosters:** Ink Veil hides you from hunters for 6 seconds; Spark Charge keeps a full, wider light for 8 seconds. Hold a booster button to see what it does.

## Purchases

Twilight, the first 12 dives, is free. *Full Descent* ($2.99, one time) opens Midnight and Abyss. *Glow Pouch* ($0.99) and *Glow Chest* ($2.99) add Ink Veils and Spark Charges; you can also trade Glimmer earned in play for boosters. Bought something before, or on another device with the same Apple Account? Open *Settings → Restore Purchases* on the home screen, or *Restore Purchases* in the Shop.

## Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
