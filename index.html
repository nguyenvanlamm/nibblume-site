Your light is bait. And a beacon.

Hold to lure

Hold Glow and small fish drift toward your light. Let go and you vanish, even from a hunter mid-dash.

Grow, don't restart

Eat smaller fish to grow through three sizes each dive. A bite above size 1 costs you a size, not the dive.

36 dives into the dark

Twilight, Midnight and Abyss. Silver fish flee light, gulpers gape before they lunge, and the Leviathan sweeps the water at the end of every zone.

How it plays

  1. Drag anywhere to swim.One thumb steers; the stick follows your finger.
  2. Hold Glow to light up.The belly lights drain while you glow and refill in the dark.
  3. Watch for red eyes, then let go.A hunter stares before it dashes. Go dark and it loses you.
  4. Eat to fill three arcs.Grow to full size to finish the dive and earn up to three stars.

Questions

Is it free?
The first zone, Twilight, is free. Full Descent ($2.99, once) opens the other two.
Are there ads?
No. There are no ads and no ad networks.
Does it need the internet?
No. It plays offline; only purchases go through the App Store.
Will I lose my progress?
Progress is saved on your device. Purchases come back with Settings → Restore Purchases.
